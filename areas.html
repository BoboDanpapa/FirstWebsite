Shinjuku
新宿区
32 listingsAvg ¥95MYield ~3.9%
Tokyo's most vibrant hub. World-class transport, entertainment, shopping and dining all in one place. Ideal for investors seeking high rental demand. 东京最繁华的中心地带。世界级交通、娱乐、购物和餐饮一应俱全，非常适合寻求高租赁需求的投资者。 東京最大の繁華街。交通・エンタメ・ショッピング・グルメが集結。高い賃貸需要を求める投資家に最適。
✨ Highlights
🚉 Major transport hub (JR, Metro)
🌳 Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden
🏢 Major business & shopping district
🍜 World-famous dining & nightlife

Shibuya
渋谷区
28 listingsAvg ¥130MYield ~3.6%
The epicenter of Tokyo youth culture, fashion and technology. Home to global brands and creative agencies. Highly sought-after for both living and investment. 东京年轻文化、时尚和科技的中心。全球品牌和创意公司云集，无论自住还是投资都极受欢迎。 東京のユース文化・ファッション・テクノロジーの中心地。グローバルブランドやクリエイティブ企業が集まる人気エリア。
✨ Highlights
🚉 Shibuya Station (major hub)
🎨 Daikanyama & Nakameguro nearby
💻 Tech startup ecosystem
🌆 Iconic Shibuya Crossing

Minato
港区
24 listingsAvg ¥280MYield ~3.2%
Tokyo's most prestigious address. Home to embassies, luxury hotels, Roppongi Hills and Tokyo Tower. The choice of diplomats, executives and discerning investors. 东京最具声望的地址。大使馆、豪华酒店、六本木之丘和东京铁塔所在地，是外交官、高管和挑剔投资者的首选。 東京で最もプレステージの高いエリア。大使館・高級ホテル・六本木ヒルズ・東京タワーが集まる外交官や経営幹部御用達の地。
✨ Highlights
🗼 Tokyo Tower views
🏛️ Roppongi Hills & Azabu Juban
🌍 Embassy district
🍽️ Michelin-starred restaurants

Setagaya
世田谷区
18 listingsAvg ¥120MYield ~3.7%
Tokyo's most family-friendly residential district. Quiet, green and safe with excellent schools. Popular with Singapore families relocating to Tokyo for a high quality of life. 东京最适合家庭居住的区域。安静、绿意盎然、安全，学校优质。深受移居东京的新加坡家庭喜爱。 東京で最もファミリー向けの住宅エリア。静かで緑豊か、安全で優れた学校も揃う。東京に移住するシンガポール人家族に人気。
✨ Highlights
🌳 Largest green space in Tokyo
🏫 Excellent international schools
🎸 Shimokitazawa culture scene
🐾 Very pet-friendly neighborhood

Chiyoda
千代田区
12 listingsAvg ¥200MYield ~3.3%
The historic heart of Tokyo. Home to the Imperial Palace, Tokyo Station and Japan's seat of government. Unmatched prestige and connectivity in the capital's core. 东京的历史中心。皇居、东京站和日本政府所在地。首都核心地带无与伦比的声望与便利性。 東京の歴史的中心地。皇居・東京駅・日本の行政機関が集まる、首都の中核エリア。
✨ Highlights
🏯 Imperial Palace & gardens
🚄 Tokyo Station (Shinkansen hub)
🏢 Marunouchi business district
🌸 Chidorigafuchi cherry blossoms

Bunkyo
文京区
9 listingsAvg ¥160MYield ~3.5%
Tokyo's academic and cultural heart. Home to Tokyo University, beautiful parks and traditional temples. A refined, intellectual neighborhood with a calm atmosphere. 东京的学术和文化中心。东京大学、美丽公园和传统寺庙所在地。氛围宁静、文化气息浓厚的精致社区。 東京の学術・文化の中心地。東京大学・美しい公園・伝統的な寺社が集まる、落ち着いた知的なエリア。
✨ Highlights
🎓 University of Tokyo campus
🌸 Famous cherry blossom spots
⚾ Tokyo Dome entertainment
🏛️ Koishikawa Korakuen Garden
